1.[Falling birth rate not due to less desire to have children](https://phys.org/news/2023-01-falling-birth-due-desire-children.html) 出生率下降的原因并不是人们生育意愿下降。就算有些人选择不生孩子，也不会对整体生育率产生实质性影响。影响更大的因素是经济和社会因素——让想生孩子的人不敢生才是症结。

2.[China Now Includes its CBDC in its Cash Circulation Statistics](https://10ztalk.com/china-now-includes-its-cbdc-in-its-cash-circulation-statistics/) 现在中国把数字人民币流通情况纳入现金流通数据统计中了。据人民银行公布的 [2022 年金融数据报告](http://www.pbc.gov.cn/en/3688247/3688978/3709137/4765043/index.html)：Starting from December 2022, e-CNY in circulation has been included in the amount of currency in circulation (M0). At end-December, e-CNY in circulation stood at RMB13.61 billion.
